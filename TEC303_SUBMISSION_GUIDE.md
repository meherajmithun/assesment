# TEC303 Assessment 2 Submission Guide

## What to Submit

The assessment requires both the complete Android Studio project and a video.

Submit this project ZIP:

- `TEC303_Assessment_2_Final.zip`

Also submit:

- One video presentation of approximately four minutes
- The required AI disclosure and prompt/response appendix

Do not submit only the APK. The lecturer needs the complete Android Studio
project contained in the ZIP.

## Check the Android App

Before submitting, confirm that:

1. The app opens correctly.
2. All four workout fields accept minutes.
3. The total is calculated automatically.
4. Reset clears all four fields and the total.
5. Invalid input shows an error and does not open the report.
6. Get Report opens the second screen after valid input.
7. The second screen shows the entered values and correct results.
8. Back or Edit Workout returns to the first screen.

The required targets are:

- Running: 60 minutes
- Cycling: 30 minutes
- Swimming: 30 minutes
- Weightlifting: 10 minutes
- Combined total: 150 minutes

## Video Presentation

Keep the video between approximately 3 minutes 36 seconds and 4 minutes 24
seconds.

Show these steps:

1. Introduce the app and show the four input fields.
2. Enter sample values and show the automatic total.
3. Press Reset and show that the values clear.
4. Demonstrate an empty or negative input error.
5. Enter valid values such as `60`, `30`, `30`, and `30`.
6. Press Get Report and show the second Activity.
7. Explain the four individual results and the overall result.
8. Explain that Intent extras pass the values between the Activities.
9. Return to the first screen.

The brief mentions an emulated environment. Using an Android Studio emulator
for the video is the safest option unless your lecturer confirms that a phone
recording is acceptable.

1. Open the TEC303 Assessment 2 submission page in MyKBS.
2. Upload `TEC303_Assessment_2_Final.zip`.
3. Upload the video file.
4. Add the required AI disclosure and prompt/response appendix.
5. Save the upload and press the final submission button.
6. Reopen the submission page and confirm all files are attached.
